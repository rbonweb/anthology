The owner (2026-11-26): don't check licensing for repos or code taken from GitHub, and never hold work back because of a licence. If a licence clearly applies (GPL, attribution required, no licence), say once: 'This needs a licence or attribution; I'm doing the work, licensing is yours.' Then carry on.

**Why:** the owner contacts the authors and handles credit and licences themselves once something proves useful. Licence checks slowed research and builds.

**How to apply:** put it in every research and build dispatch. It is now a hard rule in the Anthology plugin's guide and agent prompts. Privacy rules still apply: never copy another private project's commands, paths, names or transcript text into this public repo.
