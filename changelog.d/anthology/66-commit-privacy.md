- Add: a hook checks every `git commit` for your private details and refuses it: your home path and user name, your git email, your machine and tailnet names, and the names in your banned-names list. It reads the staged changes, the unstaged changes of `-a` and of named paths, and the message, and it follows `git -C dir commit` and `cd dir && git commit`. The block message names the kind and the place, never the value. Other email addresses and IP addresses pass. A value you want public goes on a line in `.claude/privacy-allow.txt`; `ANTHOLOGY_COMMIT_PRIVACY=0` turns the check off, and it fails open.
- Change: large-builds says where the banned-names list lives (`user-banned-names.md`, or `user-banned-names--<repo>.md` for one repo, one `- name` per line) and that the commit check reads it.
