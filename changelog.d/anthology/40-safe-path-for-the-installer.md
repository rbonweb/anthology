- Fix: when your PATH held a relative or empty entry (`.`, `::`, a leading or trailing `:`, or a folder such as `bin`), session start and the `anthology` and `anthology-mem` commands could run a `curl`, `tar`, `shasum` or `date` planted in the repository you opened while they installed the binary. They now drop those entries for their own use, keeping the absolute ones in order, and fall back to the standard system folders when none is left. The sha256 check is unchanged.
