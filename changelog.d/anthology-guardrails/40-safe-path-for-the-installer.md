- Fix: when your PATH held a relative or empty entry (`.`, `::`, a leading or trailing `:`, or a folder such as `bin`), the guard's installer could run a `curl`, `tar` or `shasum` planted in the repository you opened. The hooks now drop those entries for their own use, keeping the absolute ones in order, and fall back to the standard system folders when none is left. The sha256 check and the fail-closed behaviour are unchanged.
